Reverse Audio: Voice Flip

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip ("the app," "we," "our") is designed to let you record, reverse, and edit audio directly on your device. Your voice recordings remain under your control and are not uploaded to our servers.

This Privacy Policy explains how audio recordings are handled, what information may be processed by advertising services, how permissions and consent work, and how purchases are managed.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require an account or login. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or other identifying information to record and edit audio.

We do not collect or store your voice recordings on our servers. However, the app integrates Google AdMob, which may automatically collect or process certain information when advertising services are used.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and applicable privacy settings, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information
  • Device information, operating system, and app information
  • Advertising impressions, interactions, and performance information
  • Diagnostic information related to advertising services

This information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their applicable privacy policies. We do not receive your voice recordings through these services.

3. How Voice Recordings Are Stored

All voice recordings you create in the app are stored locally on your device. Audio recording, reversing, trimming, and playback are performed directly on your device.

Recordings are not uploaded to our servers or provided to Google AdMob or other advertising partners. The app does not provide cloud synchronization for your recordings.

Your recordings remain under your control unless you choose to share them using your device's sharing features.

4. Permissions

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip requests microphone permission when needed to record audio. Microphone access is used for audio recording, and recorded audio is processed locally.

Where applicable, the app may also request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. This permission concerns advertising-related tracking and is separate from microphone access.

You can decline tracking permission and continue using the app. Advertising may still be displayed without permission for cross-app tracking.

The app does not request direct access to the following:

  • Camera
  • Photo library
  • Contacts
  • Precise device location
  • Notifications
  • Bluetooth

Advertising services may infer approximate location from network information without requesting the device's precise location permission.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled through Apple's StoreKit 2. The app communicates with Apple's StoreKit services to retrieve product information, process purchases, restore purchases, and validate subscription status.

Payment information, including card numbers, billing details, and Apple ID credentials, is not available to us. Payment processing is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's privacy and security practices.

The app offers weekly and yearly Premium subscriptions. Premium subscribers can access the app without advertisements.

6. Advertising

The free version of Reverse Audio: Voice Flip uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertisements may include banner ads, full-screen ads, rewarded video ads, and app-open ads. Rewarded advertisements may allow free users to access certain recording functionality.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers where permitted, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in their policies.

Advertising may be personalized where applicable permissions and consent have been obtained. Otherwise, non-personalized or limited advertising may be displayed, subject to applicable requirements and ad availability.

Premium subscribers do not receive advertisements. Advertising services are not initialized for Premium subscribers under the app's current implementation.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Technologies .

7. Advertising Consent and Tracking

Where required by applicable law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present advertising privacy notices and obtain or manage consent.

Depending on your region and device settings, the app may also request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before advertising-related tracking.

If you allow tracking, advertising identifiers may be used for personalized advertising and related measurement. If you decline tracking, you can continue using the app, although advertisements may still appear.

Where available, you can review or update applicable advertising consent choices through the "Ad Privacy Options" entry in the app's Settings.

You can also manage Apple's tracking permission in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

8. Analytics and Diagnostics

The app may generate limited technical logs on your device for diagnostic purposes.

We do not operate a separate third-party analytics integration for analyzing your voice recordings. However, Google AdMob may collect advertising performance information, interactions, and diagnostics as described in Sections 2 and 6.

Your recorded audio is not transmitted to advertising or analytics providers.

9. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following external services:

  • Apple StoreKit 2: For subscription purchases, restoration, and entitlement management.
  • Google AdMob: For advertising delivery, measurement, and related services in the free version.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: For advertising consent management where applicable.

These providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account system or through uploads of voice recordings. However, third-party advertising services may process device-related information as described in this policy.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately, please contact us using the information below.

11. Data Security

Voice recordings remain stored locally on your device and benefit from the security protections provided by iOS. We do not operate servers that store your voice recordings.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to those providers' security and privacy practices.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and managing any device-level backup settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app's functionality, advertising services, privacy practices, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
