Reverse Audio: Voice Flip

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip ("the app," "we," "our") is designed around a simple principle: your voice recordings belong to you and stay on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, how permissions are used, and how purchases are handled. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip does not collect personal information or personally identifiable information (PII). The app does not require an account or login and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other identifying information in order to use the app.

3. How Voice Recordings Are Stored

All voice recordings you create in the app are stored locally on your device. Recordings are not uploaded to our servers, and the app does not provide or use cloud synchronization for your recordings. Your recordings remain under your control unless you choose to share them using your device's own sharing features.

4. Permissions

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip requests microphone permission only when needed to record audio within the app. Microphone access is used solely for audio recording, and recorded audio is processed locally on your device.

The app does not request or use any of the following:

  • Camera access
  • Photo library access
  • Contacts access
  • Location access
  • Notification permission
  • Bluetooth permission

5. Purchases

In-app purchases within Reverse Audio: Voice Flip are handled through Apple's StoreKit 2. The app communicates with Apple's StoreKit services to retrieve product information, process purchases, restore purchases, and validate subscription status. Payment information, including card numbers, billing details, and Apple ID credentials, is never available to us. Payment processing is handled directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's privacy and security practices.

6. Analytics

The app may log a limited number of technical events, such as app launches or purchase outcomes, to the device's local system log for diagnostic purposes. These logs remain on the device and are not transmitted by the app to our servers or to third-party analytics providers.

7. Third-Party Services

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip does not integrate advertising SDKs, third-party analytics services, or user-tracking technologies. The app does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework for tracking purposes. The primary external service used by the app is Apple's StoreKit, as described in Section 5. The app does not use third-party crash-reporting services.

8. Children's Privacy

Reverse Audio: Voice Flip does not knowingly collect personal information from children or users of any age. Because the app does not require an account and does not transmit voice recordings or personal information to our servers, we do not maintain personal user profiles or remotely stored user data.

9. Data Security

Voice recordings remain stored locally on your device and benefit from the security protections provided by iOS and your device. We do not operate servers that store your voice recordings or personal information. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and for managing any device-level backup settings you choose to use.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app's functionality, privacy practices, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how the app handles information.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@eebconsultancyllc.com .
